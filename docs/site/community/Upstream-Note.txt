Hi conanizer and PocketTracker contributors,

Thank you for PocketTracker. I'm Kid Bunka, and I've been working on an
independent GPL-3.0-or-later fork called Pocket Tracker Extreme for personal music
workflows on Android and the original TrimUI Brick running KNULLI. Development
has included AI assistance.

The expansion includes live synthesis using credited open DSP, studio effects,
sample editing, a kit-based groove generator and an online-source browser.
The existing physical tracker controls remain central. Original attribution,
licences and matching corresponding source are preserved.

I'd like to share the beta with interested testers in the appropriate place:
https://github.com/kidbee85-beep/Pocket-Tracker-Extreme
https://kidbee85-beep.github.io/Pocket-Tracker-Extreme/

Please let me know which channel or discussion is appropriate for a short
introduction. I'd also be glad to discuss focused fixes that could be useful
upstream. Extreme is presented as an independent fork, without implying your
endorsement or replacing the original project.

Thanks again,
Kid Bunka
