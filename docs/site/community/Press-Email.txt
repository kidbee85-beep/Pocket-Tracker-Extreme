News tip: Pocket Tracker Extreme — free open-source tracker studio for handhelds

Hello,

I'm Kid Bunka, maintainer of Pocket Tracker Extreme. I thought the project might
interest your readers: this AI-assisted development project turns an Android gaming handheld or a TrimUI Brick
running KNULLI into a button-operated music studio.

Extreme is an independent GPL-3.0-or-later fork of PocketTracker by conanizer and
contributors. It adds live synthesis with open Mutable Instruments DSP,
punch-in performance effects, detailed sample editing/slicing, kit-based groove
generation, mastering and 2,048 categorized instrument presets. A digging
browser previews supported sources and renders authorized excerpts for editing.

The beta includes a signed Android 8+ APK, a native Brick package, matching
source, an illustrated English guide and an editable original demo song. A
separate R36S Linux package is a community test. Wider device and USB-interface
compatibility is still being validated; the website distinguishes those results.
