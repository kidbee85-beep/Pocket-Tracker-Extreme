Pocket Tracker Extreme beta — a free open-source studio for Android and TrimUI Brick

Hi everyone,

I'm Kid Bunka. Pocket Tracker Extreme is an independent GPL-3.0-or-later fork of
PocketTracker by conanizer and contributors, expanded into a handheld music
studio. The original project and the DSP authors remain credited. The project has been
developed with AI assistance; app captures show the actual software interface.

The beta combines eight-track sequencing with live synth editing, sound models
adapted from Mutable Instruments, detailed sample slicing, punch-in effects,
mastering, a kit-based Drumlab/groove generator and a categorized 2,048-preset
instrument pack. The digging browser can preview supported online sources and
render an authorized excerpt into a local sample for editing.

There is an Android 8+ APK with ARM64, ARM32 and x86_64 builds, plus a standalone
TrimUI Brick/KNULLI package. An R36S Linux ZIP is a separate community test.
The preceding private Brick build had encouraging user tests. Beta.2 has software
and emulator checks; physical acceptance and wider Android/R36S reports remain open.
Direct iPhone-to-Brick USB capture failed in the current user hardware test;
separate interface routes still need testing.
M8 import supports a sampler/arrangement subset, not every M8 synth or effect.

Everything is free, including the source and features. The English illustrated
guide walks through setup, sounds, sampling and saving a first project:
https://kidbee85-beep.github.io/Pocket-Tracker-Extreme/
https://github.com/kidbee85-beep/Pocket-Tracker-Extreme
Approved beta.2 feature film (real native app capture, illustrated device frame):
https://youtu.be/wkLc71fsNU0

I'd especially appreciate reports of your device/firmware, button mapping,
sample loading, audio and saving/reopening a project. There is a structured
compatibility form in GitHub Issues. I would also love to hear music made with it.

Thanks to the PocketTracker authors and everyone whose open-source work makes
this possible.
